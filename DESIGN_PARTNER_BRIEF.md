# GovMargin design-partner brief

## What GovMargin tests

GovMargin independently reconciles a federal contractor's supported contract economics against what was cumulatively billed and paid.

The current pilot wedge is intentionally narrow:

**U.S. federal cost-reimbursement / CPFF contracts with evidenced FAR 52.216-7 applicability, settled final indirect rates, and final-voucher / closeout exposure.**

It is not a claim generator and does not assume the Government owes money.

## Ideal first design partner

Prioritize a contractor that has:

- roughly $5M–$50M of federal-contract revenue;
- at least one completed or near-closeout cost-reimbursement / CPFF contract;
- settled final indirect rates for one or more historical fiscal years;
- cumulative invoice/voucher history and payment evidence;
- finance leadership willing to validate both favorable and unfavorable findings;
- enough accounting continuity to assemble a controlled normalized dataset.

Deprioritize initially:

- FFP-only portfolios;
- T&M/LH-only portfolios;
- unresolved rate years where the pilot thesis depends on settled final rates;
- engagements requiring GovMargin to provide legal advice or DCAA representation;
- customers unwilling to surface potential overbilling/payables.

## Pilot question

For the selected contract population:

> What did the evidenced contract and settled economics support, what was cumulatively billed, what was actually paid, and what remains a supported variance, payable, no-variance item, or unknown?

## Controlled scope

Suggested first engagement:

- one contractor legal entity;
- up to five contracts / task orders;
- up to two completed fiscal years;
- one controlled normalized data package;
- final-rate / indirect-cost reconciliation;
- selected direct-cost / ODC review;
- fee, ceiling and funding checks where evidenced;
- cumulative billing and payment reconciliation;
- final-voucher / closeout review;
- human-reviewed evidence pack.

## Truth outcomes

The engagement may conclude:

1. no material variance identified;
2. supported recovery candidates identified;
3. payable / overbilling exposure identified;
4. insufficient evidence or qualified review required.

All four are valid outcomes.

## Data handling posture

Until production identity, encrypted persistence and a controlled raw-document scanning pipeline are operational, do not upload sensitive real customer documents into the public/synthetic web surface.

A design-partner engagement should begin with a data-handling plan, approved secure transfer mechanism, scoped access, retention/deletion rules and named client owners before real records are processed.

## Commercial hypothesis

Design-partner pilot hypothesis: **$7,500 fixed fee** for the controlled scope.

Do not add success-linked compensation until qualified legal/accounting review confirms permissibility and conflict safeguards.

## Validation evidence to capture

For every design-partner engagement record:

- time required to obtain usable data;
- number of contracts/years reviewed;
- total contract revenue/cost base reviewed;
- candidate and payable count;
- qualified finding rate after human review;
- verified dollar magnitude by truth state;
- time to client finance validation;
- delivery hours and direct delivery cost;
- client willingness to pay / renew;
- whether continuous monitoring would have prevented the issue;
- whether the customer authorizes an anonymized outcome statistic.

Do not publish average recovery percentages until the sample and outcome quality justify them.
