# Recovery Radar — Public Pilot Acquisition Preparation

Date: 2026-08-29

Status: `RESEARCH / NOT OUTREACH / NOT COMMERCIAL EVIDENCE`

Purpose: identify public cancellation / severe-volume-reduction triggers that could plausibly produce a closed historical supplier-side claim-pre-audit dataset. A trigger is not evidence that any named supplier has a recoverable claim, suffered a loss, submitted a claim, or would buy Recovery Radar.

## Ranking rule

A strong first historical pilot trigger should have:

1. a discrete customer program cancellation or severe reduction;
2. a known event date and enough elapsed time for a historical outcome to exist;
3. plausible program-specific supplier economics such as raw material, E&O, WIP, finished goods, tooling, NRE or supplier commitments;
4. documentary artifacts that can exist independently of Recovery Radar;
5. a potentially knowable submitted / accepted / settled / paid outcome;
6. no need to influence a live dispute.

`SUPPLIER EXPOSURE = UNKNOWN` unless directly evidenced by supplier/program records.

## Trigger shortlist

### 1. GM BrightDrop / CAMI — `PRIORITY A`

Public event:

- GM confirmed on 2025-10-21 that BrightDrop electric-van production at CAMI Assembly in Ingersoll ended and would not move to another site.
- Production had already been suspended since May 2025 because the plant was operating below capacity.
- GM's 2025 filing states EV-related impairment charges included tooling/equipment write-downs and specifically notes the October 2025 decision to stop BrightDrop production.

Why this is strong for a historical pilot:

- discrete, dated, completed production-stop event;
- enough elapsed time for internal supplier claim files or commercial outcomes potentially to exist;
- public evidence that tooling/equipment economic effects existed at OEM level.

Important boundary:

GM tooling/equipment impairment is **not** evidence of supplier recoverability or supplier claim value. A pilot is eligible only if an authorized supplier can provide its own program-specific records and known outcome.

Ideal supplier-side dataset:

`customer cancellation notice / release history → program-part mapping → inventory/WIP/FG snapshot → tooling/NRE approvals → cost records → ownership → mitigation/scrap/resale → prior credits → contract/PO/amendments → submitted claim + outcome`.

### 2. Stellantis 2025 product-plan cancellations, including planned Ram 1500 BEV — `PRIORITY A`

Public event:

- Stellantis' 2025 annual report states it cancelled certain vehicle programs, including the previously planned Ram 1500 BEV.
- The report records €9.1 billion of costs related to product-plan realignments and program cancellations.
- It separately records €6.6 billion of platform impairment charges, including €2.7 billion in property, plant and equipment, primarily tooling.

Why this is strong:

- explicit program cancellations;
- explicit tooling-heavy economic consequence at OEM level;
- likely historical evidence chain around program-specific industrialization and sourcing decisions.

Important boundary:

The €9.1B / €2.7B figures are Stellantis financial-statement amounts. They are **not supplier claim values** and must never be used as a supplier recovery estimate.

Ideal pilot profile:

A supplier with a closed 2025 cancelled-program file containing program-specific tooling/NRE or inventory commitments plus a known commercial resolution.

### 3. Ford EV program cancellations — `PRIORITY B+`

Public event:

- The report identified discontinuation of the all-electric F-150 Lightning plus cancellation of the next-generation Project T3 electric pickup and a planned next-generation electric commercial van.

Why useful:

Boundary:

Public Ford cancellation data does **not** identify supplier exposure or supplier recovery outcomes. Project T3 and other future programs may have very different supplier maturity levels, so the first gate is whether the supplier had a real sourced commitment/economic loss rather than only a forecast opportunity.

## Supplier pilot eligibility — hard gate

A historical case is `ELIGIBLE_FOR_T2_MEASUREMENT` only if all mandatory items are true:

- explicit authorization to process the case data;
- one identifiable customer/program/event with a dated cancellation or severe reduction;
- supplier had actual sourced/program-specific economic commitments before the event;
- source records exist independently of Recovery Radar;
- event is historical enough that a human-reviewed outcome can be known or explicitly marked unavailable;
- source analyst and reviewer can be different actor identities;
- no live external submission or negotiation is required for the pilot;
- currency and ownership can be represented without invented conversions/assumptions.

Reject as a first T2 case when it is only:

- forecast opportunity with no sourced commitment;
- generic industry slowdown;
- supplier relationship without program linkage;
- OEM financial impairment with no supplier-side records;
- open dispute where measurement could influence live negotiation;
- case with no independent ground truth and no meaningful historical outcome.

## Exact historical data request checklist

### Event / program

- customer cancellation / stop / reduction notice;
- event date and effective date;
- customer, program, plant, part / family identifiers;
- baseline release / forecast and revised release / forecast;
- program/source version history where available.

### Exposure

For each economic loss candidate:

- category: raw material / E&O / WIP / FG / tooling / NRE / supplier commitment / dedicated capacity / other;
- quantity;
- unit cost and currency;
- inventory/cost source record;
- ownership status and evidence;
- date/status of inventory or work at event cutoff.

### Contractual / commercial basis

- contract / terms;
- PO / sourcing award;
- amendments;
- tooling/NRE approval;
- cancellation / termination language if applicable;
- any later or competing version, kept separately rather than silently superseded.

### Mitigation / prior compensation

- resale / reuse / scrap / redeployment amount;
- mitigation evidence;
- prior credit note / reimbursement / offset;
- evidence and date for each prior compensation item.

### Historical outcome / ground truth

- supplier's human-reviewed claim lines and disposition;
- amount submitted by currency;
- amount accepted, if known;
- amount settled, if known;
- amount actually paid/credited, if known;
- dates of submission / negotiation / settlement / payment where authorized;
- reasons for exclusions or reductions if available;
- reviewer corrections and known duplicate/overclaim issues.

### Operational measurement

- analyst preparation minutes;
- reviewer minutes;
- total time to auditable package;
- evidence corrections;
- entitlement corrections;
- useful / non-useful missing-evidence actions;
- repeat-use signal;
- WTP reference only when it comes from a real decision-maker; the application will not treat a supplied note as independently verified WTP.

## Non-sent qualification positioning

`DRAFT / NOT SENT`

Recovery Radar should not lead with “we will recover money.” The first historical-pilot positioning is:

> Run one closed cancellation or severe-volume-reduction case through an independent pre-audit. Reconstruct exposure, contractual/evidence support, mitigation and prior compensation; show what would be claimable, what requires review, what is missing and what should not be claimed; then compare the result with the known historical outcome.

The pilot is valuable only if it produces measurable improvement in completeness, defensibility, review time or identification of overclaim/missed-claim risk. Synthetic accuracy is not sufficient.
