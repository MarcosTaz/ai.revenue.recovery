# GovMargin partner-channel map

## Channel thesis

Preferred partners already hold contractor trust and understand GovCon accounting/legal boundaries. GovMargin should complement rather than replace them by providing an independent, evidence-linked historical reconciliation layer.

No partner is assumed willing to refer, white-label or endorse GovMargin. The organizations below are **validation / outreach targets**, not signed partners.

## Tier 1 — GovCon accounting / finance advisers

### Redstone Government Consulting

Public evidence shows services spanning project controls, billing/invoicing, indirect-rate monitoring, incurred-cost submissions, final-rate activity and contract closeout.

Source:
- https://redstonegci.com/consulting/operational-support-services/accounting-services-support/

Partner hypothesis:
- potential referral or co-delivery channel when an independent historical reconciliation layer finds anomalies before Redstone performs qualified accounting/closeout work;
- also a strong substitute/competitor, so differentiation must be explicit.

Conflict test:
- if Redstone already offers the same independent historical earned/billed/paid recovery audit at productized scale, classify it as direct competition rather than a channel.

## Channel qualification questions

Before calling any firm a channel partner, test:

1. Do they already perform historical earned-versus-billed-versus-paid reconstruction?
2. Is the work periodic/project-based or continuously monitored?
3. Do they support multiple accounting/ERP systems without migration?
4. Would an independent evidence pack reduce their delivery time or liability?
5. Who owns the client relationship and qualified accounting/legal judgment?
6. Does the partner see GovMargin as complementary technology or competitive consulting?
7. Can referrals be structured as fixed fees without creating contingent-fee, cost-allowability or professional-practice problems?
8. Can anonymized outcome data be retained only with explicit customer permission?

## Outreach sequence

1. Validate overlap with 5–10 GovCon accountants/consultants.
2. Validate reserved-practice handoff with 2–3 Government contracts law firms.
3. Recruit one accounting design partner willing to review a synthetic evidence pack.
4. Recruit one contractor design partner through that trusted channel.
5. Measure partner-originated CAC, delivery-hour savings and review turnaround.
6. Only then test referral / white-label / co-branded models.

## Positioning sentence

> GovMargin does not replace your accounting or legal work. It independently reconstructs the contract economics and evidence trail across contracts, rate data, accounting records, billings and payments so your team can review a smaller, prioritized set of defensible variances.
