# Recovery Radar — Evidence-Qualified Historical Pilot Account Shortlist

Date: 2026-08-29

Status: `PUBLIC RESEARCH / NOT OUTREACH / NO CLAIM INFERENCE`

Purpose: identify supplier organizations with a **publicly evidenced relationship to a program that was later cancelled/ended**, so a historical pre-audit could be relevant if the supplier independently authorizes data access.

A supplier/program relationship does **not** prove stranded exposure, contractual entitlement, a submitted claim, a dispute, recovery, willingness to participate, or WTP.

## Priority 1 — Dee Zee, Inc. / GM BrightDrop

### Public relationship evidence

Public 2022 reporting states Dee Zee was selected as a Tier 1 supplier for GM BrightDrop Zevo vans, manufacturing structural/exterior components.

A Des Moines Business Record report states:

- Dee Zee was one of roughly 30 suppliers selected by BrightDrop;
- it had a 10-year BrightDrop component contract;
- a new roughly 45,000 sq ft manufacturing facility was being constructed for the program;
- an adjacent approximately 80,000 sq ft tool-and-die facility was being converted for BrightDrop component production;
- the relationship was expected to add more than 150 manufacturing jobs.

Other trade reporting states Dee Zee retooled operations, added robots and planned tens of thousands of BrightDrop structures annually.

GM later confirmed on 2025-10-21 that BrightDrop production at CAMI ended and would not be moved to another site.

### Why this is the strongest public qualification account

The public evidence establishes all of the following without inference:

`NAMED SUPPLIER`
→ `NAMED CANCELLED/ENDED PROGRAM`
→ `TIER-1 RELATIONSHIP`
→ `LONG-TERM CONTRACT REPORTED`
→ `PROGRAM-SPECIFIC FACILITY / TOOLING ADAPTATION`
→ `PROGRAM LATER ENDED`.

That is unusually close to the Recovery Radar wedge.

### Still UNKNOWN

Do **not** infer any of the following:

- that Dee Zee suffered a net economic loss;
- that dedicated assets could not be reused;
- that GM had contractual cancellation liability;
- that inventory/WIP/FG remained stranded;
- that a claim was submitted or audited;
- that any amount was accepted, settled or paid;
- that Dee Zee wants a pilot or would pay Recovery Radar.

### Exact eligibility question

A Dee Zee historical pilot becomes highly relevant only if an authorized operator can provide a closed BrightDrop-era file containing some combination of:

- GM cancellation/end-of-production notice or release history;
- BrightDrop-specific PO/sourcing/contract amendments;
- structural-component inventory/WIP/FG at cutoff;
- tooling/capital/NRE ownership and approvals;
- mitigation/redeployment/scrap evidence;
- prior credits/reimbursements;
- submitted/accepted/settled/paid historical outcome.

## Priority 2 — Magna International / Ford Project T3 / BlueOval City

### Public relationship evidence

Public 2023 Tennessee and industry announcements state Magna planned to invest more than $790M across three Tennessee facilities supporting Ford's BlueOval City second-generation electric truck program.

The planned program-specific supply included:

- battery enclosures;
- truck frames;
- complete seating / seating foam and sequencing.

Ford subsequently stated in December 2025 that it cancelled three previously planned EVs, including a full-size pickup, and reported an approximately $8.5B write-down associated with cancelled EVs and related assets.

Project T3 was the public name for Ford's next-generation full-size electric pickup planned for BlueOval City.

### Important 2026 nuance

Magna's current BlueOval City careers pages still describe active Tennessee facilities and support for Ford's next-generation truck/electric-truck activities.

Therefore the correct hypothesis is **not** “Magna has a stranded Project T3 claim.”

The correct qualification question is whether the Project T3 cancellation created any discrete historical program-specific economic delta after accounting for asset reuse, revised Ford sourcing, replacement programs and mitigation.

This makes Magna highly relevant for the Recovery Radar thesis but potentially a less clean first T2 case than Dee Zee.

### Exact eligibility question

An authorized historical file would need to distinguish:

`ORIGINAL PROJECT T3 COMMITMENT`
→ `CANCELLATION / ROADMAP CHANGE`
→ `REVISED FORD PROGRAM / ASSET REUSE`
→ `ACTUAL UNMITIGATED ECONOMIC LOSS, IF ANY`
→ `CONTRACTUAL BASIS`
→ `KNOWN OUTCOME`.

If the facilities/assets were fully repurposed with no residual supplier loss, the correct Recovery Radar result may be zero or low claimability. That is a valid and useful falsification case.

## Secondary research — BrightDrop component chain

Public SPE Automotive award material for the 2024MY Chevrolet BrightDrop identifies:

- Lear Corporation as system supplier for high-voltage bus-bar isolators;
- BASF as material supplier;
- Genesis Mold Inc. as tooling/equipment supplier.

This is useful program-link evidence but weaker for acquisition qualification than Dee Zee because the public record does not establish the commercial cancellation exposure, contract maturity or historical claim outcome for these parties.

Treat these as `RESEARCH_ONLY` until stronger direct program-commercial evidence exists.
