# Recovery Radar — Historical Pilot Acquisition Plan

Status: `PREPARED / NOT SENT / NO EXTERNAL CONTACT AUTHORIZED`

Purpose: identify public evidence that a supplier has experienced exactly the kind of program cancellation / severe volume reduction Recovery Radar is built to pre-audit, then qualify a **closed or historical case** without assuming entitlement, recoverability, data access or willingness to participate.

This file is acquisition preparation only. It does not authorize outreach, claim submission, negotiation, spending or external data collection.

Use `PILOT_ROUTING_MATRIX.md` for the separate question of how an authorized inquiry could be routed. **Case fit and routing fit are not the same score.**

## Selection rule

Prefer a historical case with all of the following:

1. customer program cancellation or material volume reduction is publicly or internally evidenced;
2. supplier-side stranded cost / tooling / engineering / pre-production exposure is evidenced;
3. a recovery claim, commercial recovery or settlement is known where possible;
4. the matter is closed or sufficiently historical that analysis cannot influence a live dispute;
5. the supplier could, if authorized, provide normalized internal records for an independent ground-truth comparison;
6. known historical outcome can be distinguished from entitlement truth: settlement amount is a useful observed outcome, not proof every modeled entitlement was legally correct.

## Ranked historical-pilot candidates — measurement quality

### 1 — Dauch Corporation / former American Axle & Manufacturing Holdings (AAM)

**Classification:** `HIGHEST-FIT CLOSED HISTORICAL CASE`

Public evidence:

- In April 2024, one of AAM's largest customers terminated production purchase orders related to a previously announced contract to supply e-Beam axles for a future vehicle program.
- AAM publicly stated that it submitted a cancellation claim to recover costs incurred in connection with those terminated purchase orders.
- At December 31, 2024, AAM publicly disclosed approximately **US$70 million of balance-sheet assets associated with the program**, consisting of capitalized engineering, design and development costs and other commercial amounts, and said it believed it was entitled to claim and recover the full amount while also warning that ultimate recovery was not determinable and could differ materially.
- In January 2026, the company reached a final settlement agreement with the customer.
- The 2025 annual report said it expected approximately **US$28 million** in Q1 2026 reimbursement of capitalized engineering, design and development costs; it also recorded a **US$20 million** write-off of certain assets not recovered under the agreement and a separate approximately **US$22 million** write-off tied to an asset with a substantially offsetting liability.
- Effective January 26, 2026, American Axle & Manufacturing Holdings, Inc. changed its name to Dauch Corporation. The filings do not publicly identify the customer behind the cancelled e-Beam program; do not infer one.

Critical comparison boundary:

**Do not describe this publicly as a “US$70M claim reduced to a US$28M settlement.”**

The public US$70M figure was a balance-sheet asset amount associated with the program, not a disclosed line-level original claim amount. The US$28M figure was specifically described as reimbursement of capitalized engineering, design and development costs, while other write-offs/accounting entries were also recorded. Without the authorized historical claim file and category-level settlement allocation, the figures are not proven apples-to-apples.

Why strongest:

- exact cancellation-claim workflow;
- historical/closed settlement;
- public pre-settlement exposure plus final settlement/write-off accounting creates a strong falsification target;
- engineering/design/development and other commercial cost categories test entitlement/evidence boundaries, not only stranded inventory;
- authorized internal records could test whether Recovery Radar reconstructs recoverable/non-recoverable category splits without tuning to the final outcome.

Still UNKNOWN:

- whether a usable line-level historical file is retained;
- original line-level claim amount/categories;
- category allocation of settlement/write-offs;
- reasons for accepted/excluded amounts;
- data-sharing authorization;
- reviewer/process burden;
- willingness to participate/pay.

## First-pilot qualification gate

A candidate should enter a data-access conversation only if a knowledgeable supplier-side participant can answer **YES** to the first four questions and no stop condition applies.

1. Was there a specific cancelled/terminated program or documented severe volume reduction?
2. Did the supplier incur customer/program-specific cost before or during the change?
3. Is there a historical record connecting the program event to the affected cost categories?
4. Is the matter closed/historical or otherwise explicitly authorized for retrospective analysis?
5. Is at least one contractual/commercial basis document available for review, even if the ultimate conclusion was non-recoverable?
6. Are mitigation, reuse/resale/scrap and prior-reimbursement records available where relevant?
7. Is there an original claim/submission or internal recovery analysis against which to compare?
8. Is there a known historical outcome: rejected, accepted, settled and/or paid amount?
9. Can an independent reviewer explain the historical decision without being shown Recovery Radar's expected result first?
10. Can the records be shared in a controlled local pilot without exposing unrelated customer/program data?

## Stop conditions

Do not onboard the case if:

- the only evidence is a public OEM cancellation with no supplier-specific exposure;
- the dispute is live and the pilot could influence submission/negotiation without explicit authorization;
- source cost/inventory/tooling/engineering records cannot be separated from unrelated programs;
- the case depends primarily on physical verification that cannot be represented or independently evidenced;
- currencies/categories have already been manually combined in a way that cannot be reconstructed;
- the desired outcome is to make Recovery Radar justify a predetermined claim amount;
- the historical ground truth cannot be prepared independently enough to measure false positives/false negatives;
- the supplier expects the software to provide a legal opinion or autonomous liability determination.

## Minimum historical data request — only after participation is authorized

### Program event

- customer/program identifier or sanitized stable alias;
- cancellation/termination/volume-reduction notice and date;
- baseline forecast/release/PO and revised/cancelled state;
- affected part/program identifiers.

### Economic exposure

Per economic-loss line:

- category;
- quantity;
- unit cost and source currency;
- inventory/cost ledger reference;
- ownership evidence;
- customer-specific versus reusable status;
- mitigation/reuse/resale/scrap amount + evidence;
- prior reimbursement/credit amount + evidence;
- links to program and cost center where available.

### Contractual/commercial basis

- relevant purchase order / award / supply agreement / cancellation clause;
- amendments/version history that existed at the event date;
- supporting approval or customer communication relied on by the historical reviewer;
- category-specific caps/exclusions if applicable;
- do not pre-label a later document as superseding unless a human reviewer can establish applicability.

### Historical outcome / ground truth

- original amount submitted, if any;
- claimed categories/lines;
- customer/auditor challenged or rejected lines/reasons;
- revised claim(s);
- settlement amount and date;
- payment evidence if paid;
- internal write-off/non-recoverable lines;
- analyst preparation time if reconstructable;
- reviewer time/corrections if reconstructable.

## Measurement protocol after authorization

1. retain the actual human/data-owner authorization outside the app;
2. normalize the bundle and concrete evidence files;
3. run `scope-manifest` and preserve `source_scope_sha256` + artifact hash;
4. atomically import and independently review evidence/entitlement interpretations;
5. produce deterministic claim package/export;
6. freeze independent `RECOVERY_RADAR_GROUND_TRUTH_V1`, recording the matching source-scope hash and ground-truth method;
7. run `score-historical`; scope mismatch must fail before score output;
8. preserve `RECOVERY_RADAR_T2_SCORECARD_V1` and final SHA-256;
9. measure FP/FN, state/currency mismatches, amount error, reviewer corrections, missing-evidence usefulness and time-to-package;
10. compare historical claim/settlement/payment only as a separate observed outcome;
11. classify any material miss as P0/P1/P2 and repair before terminal promotion;
12. T2 promotion remains a Factory evidence decision, never an application field.
