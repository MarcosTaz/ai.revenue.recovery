# Aviation Recovery — First-Wave Outreach

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-04_

## Positioning rule

Do not lead with AI, automation, SaaS or a claim that the airline is leaking money. Lead with an auditable control question and a bounded pilot.

## Wizz Air — CFO / Finance Operations

**Subject:** Maintenance reserve recovery control

Veronika,

How does Wizz currently prove that every maintenance-reserve reimbursement and lessor contribution generated by recent maintenance events was claimed correctly, on time and with all eligible cost captured?

We run a bounded independent recovery scan across the governing lease terms, MRO evidence, reserve/contribution statements and prior settlements. The output is a reconciled candidate register and claim-ready evidence package — not a lease-management replacement.

A first proof can be limited to a small engine or heavy-maintenance cohort.

## Follow-up 1 — 4–5 business days

**Subject:** Re: maintenance entitlement recovery

A short follow-up on this.

The useful starting point is not a large data project. One recent shop visit, heavy check or small lease cohort is enough to test whether contract terms, eligible cost, reserve/contribution balances and prior settlements reconcile cleanly.

If the answer is “nothing was missed,” the pilot still produces evidence-based assurance rather than a speculative recovery number.

## Discovery-call objective

Do not sell the whole product. Obtain agreement on one controlled proof case and identify:

- asset / engine cohort;
- maintenance event window;
- relevant entitlement family;
- governing lease package owner;
- finance/technical evidence owner;
- prior-claims register owner;
- internal reviewer who can approve an external claim package.

The target outcome of the first call is permission to scope a paid Recovery Scan.
