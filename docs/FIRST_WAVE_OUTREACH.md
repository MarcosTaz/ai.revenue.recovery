# Aviation Recovery — First-Wave Outreach

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-04_

## Positioning rule

Do not lead with AI, automation, SaaS or a claim that the airline is leaking money. Lead with an auditable control question and a bounded paid pilot.

The first commercial objective is not a platform demo. It is agreement to scope one **Recovery Scan**.

## 1. TAP Air Portugal — CFO

**Subject:** Lease entitlement recovery scan

Renato,

Given your Corporate Finance & Risk background, how does TAP currently prove that every lease-linked maintenance reimbursement, lessor contribution and selected redelivery adjustment generated by a defined event cohort was captured, evidenced and settled correctly?

We run a bounded independent recovery scan across the governing lease terms, maintenance evidence, financial records and prior settlements. The output separates claim-ready value from unsupported potential and produces a defensible recovery register — without replacing existing systems.

I would start with 3–10 aircraft or engines and one recent event family.

**CTA:** Would a 20-minute scoping call be useful to test whether one cohort is suitable?

## Discovery-call objective

Do not sell the whole product. Obtain agreement on one controlled paid proof case and identify:

- asset / engine cohort;
- maintenance or redelivery event window;
- relevant entitlement family;
- governing lease package owner;
- finance/technical evidence owner;
- prior-claims register owner;
- internal reviewer who can approve an external claim package;
- procurement path for a bounded Recovery Scan.

The target outcome of the first call is permission to issue a scoped paid Recovery Scan proposal.
