# GovMargin Competitive Benchmark — 2026

## Objective

GovMargin does not compete by replacing ERP/accounting systems. It competes by independently reconstructing supported contract economics and reconciling earned, billed and paid amounts with evidence, deterministic calculations and bilateral truth.

## Benchmark set

- Deltek Costpoint: lifecycle contract management, centralized contract data, closeout coverage, project financial integration.
- Unanet GovCon ERP: real-time project visibility, indirect-rate management, billing guardrails, synchronized financial/project data, audit trail orientation.
- GovCon accounting/compliance ecosystem: incurred-cost support, provisional/final indirect rates, DCAA readiness, timekeeping and billing controls.

## Features GovMargin should absorb

1. Centralized contract register with version/modification lineage.
2. Real-time margin and billing status.
3. Indirect-rate monitoring and provisional-vs-final true-up.
4. Billing guardrails and invoice/payment reconciliation.
5. Complete audit trail.
6. Closeout workflow and final-voucher readiness.
7. Role-scoped human review.
8. Exception-first dashboards.
9. Data ingestion independent of source ERP.
10. Partner-ready reporting and evidence packs.

## Features GovMargin must not copy blindly

- ERP replacement.
- Generic project management.
- Payroll/timekeeping as a system of record.
- AI-generated entitlement assertions.
- Automatic rate or allowability decisions without source-linked rules.
- Recovery promotion where funding, ceiling, release, procedural or evidence states are unresolved.

## White-space retained

GovMargin's defensible wedge is the independent reconciliation layer:

CONTRACT + RULES + COSTS + RATES + BILLINGS + PAYMENTS
→ SUPPORTED ECONOMICS
→ BILATERAL VARIANCE
→ HUMAN-REVIEWED ACTION

The system must be equally willing to produce a contractor receivable, a contractor payable/overbilling exposure, no variance, or UNKNOWN.

## Premium capabilities to build

- Contract Economic Twin: a versioned economic representation of each contract/order/modification.
- Recovery Confidence Graph: every candidate linked to clause, regulatory version, transaction, rate, ceiling, funding and review state.
- Closeout Risk Radar: final-rate, final-voucher, release, funding and deadline exceptions.
- Continuous Margin Assurance: live EARNED / BILLED / PAID / UNBILLED / AT RISK / NOT ALLOWABLE / UNKNOWN.
- M&A Economic Rights & Exposure Schedule.
- Quick-closeout eligibility screener with explicit FAR 42.708 constraints.
- Evidence integrity and provenance controls before parsing/model use.
- Cash application anomaly detection for unapplied/short/over payments.
- Portfolio view for PE/partners without cross-tenant data leakage.

## Product rule

A competitor feature is adopted only when it improves at least one of:
- economic truth,
- false-positive suppression,
- evidence quality,
- operator speed,
- closeout readiness,
- cash recovery,
- recurring monitoring value,
- partner distribution.

GovMargin remains recovery-first, independent, fail-closed and source-linked.
