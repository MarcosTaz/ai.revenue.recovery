## Short company/service description

Aviation Recovery is an assisted, evidence-led commercial recovery service for airlines and aircraft lessees. It reconstructs lease-linked economic entitlements from governing contract terms, maintenance-event evidence, reserve/contribution records and prior settlements, then distinguishes unsupported potential from claim-ready value and verified recovery.

The initial focus is maintenance-reserve reimbursements and lessor maintenance contributions, with bounded expansion to cost-sharing, security-deposit/prepaid-rent releases and selected end-of-lease adjustments.

## Value proposition for TAP

TAP can use a bounded Recovery Scan to independently prove whether a selected aircraft or engine event cohort reconciles end-to-end across:

- governing lease / amendment terms;
- maintenance event and workscope evidence;
- invoices and eligible-cost logic;
- reserve / contribution balances;
- prior submissions and settlements;
- deadlines, exclusions, caps and deductibles;
- final economic settlement evidence.

The engagement does not require replacement of TAP systems or an enterprise integration to prove initial value.

## Recommended first engagement

**Recovery Scan — assisted pilot**

Suggested scope:

- 3–10 aircraft and/or engines;
- one recent maintenance, shop-visit or redelivery event family;
- 12–24 months of bounded evidence where appropriate;
- 1–3 entitlement families;
- human-reviewed externalization of any claim package.

## Deliverables

1. Contract / entitlement rule map.
2. Source and evidence inventory with lineage gaps.
3. Deterministic entitlement calculations.
4. Candidate register separated into blocked, review-required and claim-ready value.
5. False-positive / double-recovery challenge log.
6. Claim-ready dossier for items that pass the economic and evidence gates.
7. Executive closeout separating claim-ready, submitted/approved and verified settled recovery.

## Commercial model

Indicative assisted-pilot ranges already approved in the Aviation Recovery commercial model:

- Recovery Scan: **EUR 7,500–15,000**;
- Forensic Reconciliation / dossier work: **EUR 15,000–50,000+** depending on scope;
- optional success component only when separately agreed and defined against **verified recovered value**;
- monitoring after a successful pilot: **EUR 2,500–10,000+/month**.

A procurement submission should use a fixed bounded scope where possible rather than presenting all price bands at once.

## Security / confidentiality baseline

- minimum-necessary data collection;
- synthetic data for demonstration by default;
- governing source files preserved without silent alteration;
- per-claim evidence lineage;
- human release gate before any external claim package;
- no customer data used for model training;
- settlement requires verifiable evidence;
- retention and access rules agreed for each paid engagement;
- no representation as legal counsel, external auditor or airworthiness authority.

## Acceptance criteria for first pilot

The pilot succeeds if it demonstrates all of the following:

- contract logic is reproducible;
- each economic candidate has traceable source evidence;
- prior claims / payments are reconciled to avoid double recovery;
- unsupported candidates are blocked rather than inflated;
- any claim-ready output can withstand human review;
- recovered value is reported only after settlement evidence exists;
- TAP receives a usable control register even if the bounded cohort reveals no missed recovery.
