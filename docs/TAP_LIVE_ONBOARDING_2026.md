# TAP live supplier onboarding — verified 2026-09-04

## Purpose

This file freezes the currently verified external onboarding path for the preferred first-reference account, TAP Air Portugal. It must be used instead of rediscovering or guessing the supplier-registration route.

## Official path

TAP states that companies wishing to collaborate must complete supplier onboarding and that supplier information is reviewed and recorded in TAP's supplier database.

The official TAP FAQ publishes a buyer-specific self-registration route:

`https://tap.supplier-eu.ariba.com/ad/selfRegistration/_c_/C2`

TAP states that the company cannot be reviewed/processed as a supplier until registration and the subsequent questionnaire are complete.

For general supplier-process questions, TAP publishes:

`suppliersupport@tap.pt`

## Ariba sequence

1. Start from TAP's buyer-specific self-registration route, not from a generic standalone Ariba account.
2. Create or use an SAP Ariba / SAP Business Network supplier account.
3. Complete the TAP supplier registration questionnaire.
4. Use the primary business address if the supplier has more than one address; additional addresses can be added later in the company profile.
5. Submit the complete response. Saving alone does not submit the questionnaire.
6. TAP validates the information and finalizes supplier registration.
7. Ensure the TAP relationship request is accepted in the Ariba customer-relationship area.

## Cost / account baseline
