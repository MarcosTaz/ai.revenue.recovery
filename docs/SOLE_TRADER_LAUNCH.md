# AERVIND — Initial Sole-Trader Launch Model

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-04_

## Decision

AERVIND will **not create a Portuguese company before first revenue** unless a specific customer procurement process makes a company mandatory.

Initial contracting and invoicing model:

- commercial brand: **AERVIND**;
- product: **Aviation Lease Entitlement Recovery**;
- offer: **Recovery Scan**;
- legal supplier for initial engagements: the existing Portuguese individual professional / sole-trader activity of the operator, using the operator's real legal name, tax number and registered professional address;
- AERVIND may be shown as the commercial brand, but invoices/contracts must preserve the legally required supplier identity;
- future scale vehicle: **AERVIND, Unipessoal Lda.**, only when revenue, liability, procurement or tax/accounting complexity justifies it.

This decision removes company-formation cost from the pre-revenue critical path.

## Portuguese activity classification

Current working classification for the AERVIND service is:

**CAE Rev.4 70200 — Atividades de consultoria para os negócios e outra consultoria para a gestão**.

This is the 2025+ successor to CAE Rev.3 70220. It fits the current service as business / financial-management consulting and assisted reconciliation, not legal, tax, audit, MRO or airworthiness work.

Before the first paid AERVIND engagement, verify that the operator's Portuguese activity registration already contains an adequate CAE/CIRS. If not, add/alter it through the Portal das Finanças before providing the service. The change itself is an administrative truth gate, not a product gate.

## Contract / invoice identity rule

Customer-facing presentation may use:

**AERVIND**
Aviation Lease Entitlement Recovery

But legal documents must identify the actual supplier, for example:

`[REAL LEGAL NAME], Portuguese tax no. [REAL NIF], trading as AERVIND`

Never invent or suppress the legally required supplier name, tax number or address.

## International B2B invoicing baseline

AERVIND is global from day one. The Portuguese sole-trader vehicle does not limit the customer geography.

Before each invoice:

1. identify the real contracting customer entity;
2. collect its legal name, billing address and tax/VAT identifier where applicable;
3. determine the correct VAT treatment for the customer's country and tax status;
4. for EU B2B services falling under the general place-of-supply rule, validate the customer's VAT status and apply the appropriate reverse-charge treatment / reporting obligations;
5. issue the invoice through a compliant Portuguese invoicing route;
6. retain contract, invoice, payment and customer-tax evidence.

Do not hard-code one VAT treatment for every international customer.

## Minimum cost before first sale

Required specifically to create AERVIND as a commercial operation:

- company incorporation: **EUR 0 now**;
- share capital: **EUR 0 now**;
- mandatory company accountant: **EUR 0 now**, assuming the operator remains validly within the applicable individual-professional regime;
- company bank account: **EUR 0 now**;
- AERVIND-specific software subscription: **EUR 0 required**;
- commercial domain: optional legally, but selected for credibility and launch.

Taxes, social-security contributions and any insurance obligations of the underlying individual professional activity remain real operating obligations; they are not AERVIND incorporation costs.

## Domain

Primary domain selected: **aervind.com**.

Availability was rechecked on 2026-09-04 at USD 11.25 / year through the available registrar check. Purchase is a real payment side effect and must be completed through an authorized registrar account.

## First-sale readiness gate

AERVIND may begin commercial outreach immediately.

A paid engagement may be accepted when:

- the operator's real individual-professional activity is active and correctly classified for the service;
- the customer accepts contracting with the individual legal supplier trading as AERVIND;
- the SOW clearly states the service boundary;
- invoicing/VAT treatment is verified for that customer;
- any customer-specific procurement, insurance or compliance requirements are satisfied.

A Portuguese Lda. is **not** a default precondition.

## Enterprise procurement exception

Some airlines may require a registered company, specific insurance, vendor certifications, or company-only onboarding fields. In that case:

- do not misstate the individual supplier as a company;
- do not invent a NIPC or corporate registration;
- either use a procurement path that accepts an individual supplier or create AERVIND, Unipessoal Lda. when the commercial opportunity justifies it.

TAP remains a preferred reference target, but formal TAP onboarding must follow the actual supplier type accepted by its live questionnaire.

## Revenue trigger for reconsidering incorporation

Reassess creation of **AERVIND, Unipessoal Lda.** when one or more of these occur:

- a qualified buyer requires a company to contract;
- professional-liability / contractual exposure makes separation prudent;
- recurring AERVIND revenue makes company accounting economically sensible;
- hiring/subcontracting, banking or payment operations require a separate entity;
- tax/accounting advice supports the change based on actual revenue and costs.

Until then, optimize for first proof and first cash, not corporate overhead.
