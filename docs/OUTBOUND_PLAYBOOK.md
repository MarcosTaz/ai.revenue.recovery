## Commercial message

Do not lead with AI, a lease-management replacement, a broad transformation pitch, or an allegation that the target missed money.

Lead with one finance/lease-control question grounded in the target's own public facts:

**Can every eligible lease entitlement in the selected cohort be traced from the governing clause through event evidence, claim history and final settlement?**

Public information can justify that question. It does not prove leakage, entitlement, under-claiming or recoverable value.

## Best account signals

Prioritize operators with one or more publicly supportable signals:

- meaningful leased aircraft or spare-engine exposure;
- maintenance reserves or lessor-maintenance contribution economics;
- recent/upcoming shop visits, heavy checks or engine events;
- lease exits, redeliveries, purchases of previously leased aircraft or amendments;
- multiple lessors / heterogeneous lease structures;
- fleet transition or other situations where contract, technical and finance evidence must reconcile across owners.

Do not infer internal process weakness, spreadsheet reliance or missed amounts without evidence.

## Discovery questions

Qualify a Recovery Scan without demanding data on the first call:

1. Who owns the selected entitlement family today?
2. What asset/event cohort would be small enough to test independently?
3. Where is the governing lease/amendment version held?
4. What technical evidence establishes the triggering event?
5. Where are reserve/contribution statements and scoped finance evidence held?
6. Is prior claim / approval / settlement history available for the cohort?
7. How are contractual notice and deadline requirements controlled?
8. How does management distinguish identified, review-required, claim-ready, submitted/approved and settled value?
9. Who would own factual questions during a 10-business-day scan?
10. What decision would management make if the cohort closes cleanly or produces evidence-backed exceptions?

## Qualification gate

A potential engagement is qualified only when there is:

- a defined asset/event population;
- one defined entitlement family;
- governing contract evidence available under an agreed confidentiality boundary;
- a real finance/fleet/lease-management owner;
- prior claims/settlements can be reconciled in scope;
- a bounded decision point after the scan;
- truthful supplier/procurement path is feasible.

Public-source research alone does not make an account pilot-qualified.

## Proof language

Use these distinctions consistently:

- **identified potential** — positive formula economics before all release gates pass;
- **blocked potential** — material rule/evidence issue prevents release;
- **review-required** — human exception/review remains;
- **claim-ready value** — required economic, evidence-lineage and human-review gates pass;
- **submitted / acknowledged / disputed / approved / rejected** — counterparty lifecycle states, not recovered value;
- **verified recovered value** — positive settlement evidence with amount, currency and reference.

## Objection handling

### “We already have lease-management software.”

AERVIND is an independent recovery/control layer for a bounded cohort. The first scan tests whether contractual economics can be evidenced through final settlement; it does not ask the airline to replace its system of record.

### “Our team already manages claims.”

The scan provides independent reconciliation. A useful outcome can be evidence-backed exceptions **or** defensible assurance that the selected cohort closes correctly.

### “We cannot share the full portfolio.”

AERVIND does not ask for the full portfolio. The canonical first scan is capped at five aircraft/engines, one entitlement family and twelve months of selected events, using a minimum-necessary evidence set.

### “Do you need access to our systems?”

No live access is required for the first scan. Use approved PDFs, spreadsheets and controlled exports.

### “Can you guarantee recovery?”

No. Contract language, evidence, deadlines, offsets and counterparty position determine the result. AERVIND applies deterministic calculation, source-lineage and human-review controls; it does not promise a predetermined recovery outcome.

### “What if you find nothing?”

A correctly evidenced no-claim / controls-close result is valid. The buyer receives assurance on the tested cohort rather than a manufactured positive claim.
