# Aviation Recovery — Assisted Pilot GTM

_Last updated: 2026-09-04_

## Commercial thesis

Aviation Recovery should not enter the market as another lease-management platform. Established products already centralize lease/asset data, model maintenance cashflows, and manage reserve balances. The sharper wedge is **lessee-side recovery execution**: locate missed contractual entitlements, reconstruct the calculation, assemble evidence, attack false positives, and produce a claim-ready dossier tied to settlement truth.

## ICP

Priority accounts:

- airlines / operators with meaningful leased fleet or spare-engine exposure;
- operators still using spreadsheets, calendar reminders, disconnected M&E/ERP/finance data or outsourced lease administration;
- airlines with recent or upcoming engine shop visits, heavy checks, redeliveries or lease amendments;
- smaller/mid-size operators without deep in-house lease-claims capacity;
- larger airline groups where even low miss-rates across many leases create material working-capital leakage.

Priority functions:

- CFO / Finance leadership
- Fleet / Aircraft Asset Management
- Engineering Finance / Powerplant Finance
- Lease Management
- Procurement / Strategic Sourcing
- Internal Audit / Commercial Assurance

## Entry offer

### 1. Recovery Scan

A bounded review of a selected fleet subset, lease subset or maintenance-event cohort.

Deliverables:

- entitlement inventory;
- deadline / time-bar heatmap;
- evidence-gap register;
- high/medium/low recovery candidates;
- conservative gross recovery range;
- prioritized next actions.

Suggested assisted pricing: **€7.5k–€15k** depending on volume and document complexity.

### 2. Forensic Reconciliation

For selected candidates:

- governing lease/amendment reconstruction;
- eligible-cost build-up;
- reserve/contribution ledger reconciliation;
- exclusions/caps/floors/deductibles;
- prior claims and settlements;
- deterministic calculation trace;
- red-team review.

Suggested pricing: **€15k–€50k+** per portfolio workstream, with optional success component where legally/commercially appropriate.

### 3. Claim Dossier + Settlement Tracking

Claim-ready evidence package, submission register, counterparty response log, negotiated changes, and final economic-settlement evidence.

Suggested success component: **10–20% of verified recovered value**, subject to engagement structure and jurisdiction.

### 4. Recovery Monitoring

Recurring review of new maintenance events, approaching claim deadlines, reserve/contribution positions and unresolved claims.

Suggested pricing: **€2.5k–€10k+/month** based on fleet size and service depth.

## Pilot scope

A strong first pilot is intentionally narrow:

- 3–10 leased aircraft/engines;
- 1–3 entitlement families;
- 12–24 months of recent maintenance events;
- one agreed source package: leases/amendments, reserve statements, MRO invoices, workscope/technical completion evidence, prior claims, settlements and relevant finance ledger exports.

## Pilot acceptance criteria

Pilot is commercially successful when it produces all of the following:

1. Every reviewed candidate has a governing rule/version.
2. Every material number can be traced to evidence.
3. Known prior claims/payments are reconciled.
4. Deadlines and evidence gaps are explicit.
5. False positives are rejected with reasons.
6. At least one output reaches `claim_ready` or the pilot proves, with evidence, why no recoverable value exists.
7. Executive summary quantifies verified recovery, blocked potential recovery, unresolved exposure and next deadlines.

## Differentiation

Position against broad lease/asset-management systems on outcome, not breadth:

- **Recovery-first** rather than portfolio administration first.
- **Lessee-side economic entitlement** rather than predominantly lessor asset management.
- **Evidence lineage** for every material calculation input.
- **Human-review gate** before claim-ready status.
- **False-recovery red team** to reduce embarrassing or duplicate claims.
- **Settlement truth**: submitted is not recovered; cash/credit/offset evidence closes the loop.
- **Assisted deployment first**: no requirement for a large transformation program before proving value.

## Initial outbound message

Lead with a concrete operational question rather than generic AI positioning:

> How are maintenance-reserve reimbursements and lessor contributions reconciled today across lease terms, MRO invoices, technical evidence and prior settlements — and how do you prove no eligible claim was missed?

The first call should aim to identify one recent engine shop visit/heavy maintenance event or one lease cohort suitable for a confidential recovery scan.

## Sales proof pack required before broad outbound

- deterministic engine demo using synthetic lease rules;
- one claim dossier example;
- one executive recovery summary;
- red-team checklist;
- data-request checklist;
- confidentiality/security one-pager;
- engagement scope and commercial terms;
- explicit statement that contract interpretation remains human-reviewed and source-grounded.
