## Commercial thesis

AERVIND should not enter the market as another lease-management platform. The initial wedge is **lessee-side entitlement recovery and control**: reconstruct what the governing lease allows, reconcile it against maintenance/finance evidence and prior claims, reject false positives, and produce an auditable candidate/dossier tied to settlement truth.

The first buyer is not being asked to replace ERP, M&E, lease-management or banking systems. AERVIND begins with a small exported evidence cohort.

## ICP

Priority accounts:

- airlines/operators with meaningful leased aircraft or spare-engine exposure;
- operators with maintenance reserves, lessor contributions, deposit/prepaid-rent or end-of-lease economics;
- airlines with recent/upcoming shop visits, heavy checks, lease exits, redeliveries, amendments or lease-purchase transitions;
- finance/fleet organizations where the economic chain crosses several owners or systems.

Priority buying group:

- CFO / Finance leadership;
- Fleet / Aircraft Asset Management;
- Engineering Finance / Powerplant Finance;
- Lease Management;
- Internal Audit / Commercial Assurance;
- Procurement / Strategic Sourcing after an internal sponsor exists.

Indicative **EUR 2,500–10,000+/month** only after repeat value is proven and the customer actually requires recurring monitoring.

## Differentiation

- **Recovery/control first**, not broad portfolio administration.
- **Lessee-side economics**, not primarily lessor asset management.
- **Evidence lineage** for material calculation inputs.
- **Human review** before claim-ready externalization.
- **False-recovery attacks** before a positive candidate leaves review.
- **Settlement truth**: submitted or approved is not recovered.
- **Low-friction first engagement** using controlled documents/exports instead of a transformation project.
