# AERVIND — Recovery Scan Scope of Work

_Status: canonical launch SOW for the first paid engagement._

## 1. Purpose

AERVIND will perform a bounded, evidence-led reconciliation of selected aircraft and/or engine lease entitlements to determine whether the agreed cohort closes cleanly from governing contract terms through event evidence, financial evidence, prior claims and settlement history.

AERVIND does not assume that leakage exists. A clean reconciliation is a valid outcome when the evidence supports it.

## 2. Canonical launch scope

The standard first paid Recovery Scan covers:

- up to **5 aircraft and/or engines**;
- **1 primary entitlement family**;
- up to **12 months** of selected events;
- the governing lease and relevant amendments for each in-scope asset;
- relevant reserve / lessor-contribution statements;
- relevant maintenance-event evidence, invoices, workscope and completion evidence;
- known prior claim, reimbursement and settlement records within scope;
- only the scoped finance extracts needed to reconcile those events.

The parties may substitute an equivalent bounded cohort before signature when it provides a better first proof case. Any expansion after scope lock requires written agreement.

### No live system access required

The launch Recovery Scan is designed to operate from **PDFs, spreadsheets and controlled exports** supplied through the agreed data-transfer route.

AERVIND does not require credentials or live access to the customer's ERP, M&E, lease-management, banking or other operational systems for this first engagement.

## 3. Entitlement family

The first scan uses one primary family, selected from a source-grounded use case such as:

- maintenance-reserve reimbursement;
- lessor maintenance contribution / cost-sharing;
- security-deposit or prepaid-rent release;
- selected end-of-lease adjustment.

Lease-specific rules are reconstructed from the actual governing documents. Missing or ambiguous terms are not replaced with favorable assumptions.

## 4. Deliverables

AERVIND will provide:

1. **Scope and source inventory** — in-scope assets/events and supplied evidence index.
2. **Contract / entitlement rule map** — governing rule version, formula, caps/floors/deductibles, exclusions, evidence requirements and deadlines where determinable.
3. **Deterministic reconciliation** — reproducible calculation against supplied event and finance evidence.
4. **Candidate register** — each candidate assigned an explicit economic status.
5. **Evidence / lineage register** — material source references and unresolved gaps.
6. **Deadline / time-bar view** — where the supplied governing terms permit determination.
7. **False-positive / duplicate-recovery challenge** — prior claim/payment and exclusion checks.
8. **Claim-ready dossier/package** — only for candidates that pass economic, source-lineage and human-review gates.
9. **Executive closeout** — mutually exclusive recovery-truth buckets and recommended next actions.
10. **Portable assisted case/audit export** — where the AERVIND Operator Console is used for the engagement.

## 5. Recovery-truth standard

Economic status and external lifecycle remain separate.

Economic status:

`blocked / review_required / claim_ready / zero_entitlement`

External lifecycle:

`detected -> reviewing -> claim_ready -> submitted -> acknowledged / disputed / approved / rejected -> settled`

AERVIND reports recovered value only when supported by settlement evidence such as cash receipt, credit memo, refund or contractually valid offset.

Identified, blocked, review-required, claim-ready, submitted or approved amounts are not reported as recovered cash.

## 6. Customer responsibilities

The customer will:

- provide the agreed bounded evidence cohort through the approved transfer/storage route;
- identify a finance, fleet, lease-management or technical-finance owner able to resolve factual questions;
- identify the governing contract/amendment set for the selected assets or help resolve version uncertainty;
- confirm material contract interpretations when language remains ambiguous after source review;
- provide known prior claim/reimbursement/settlement evidence in scope;
- review and approve any external claim package before a real submission;
- perform or authorize any real external submission through the agreed channel;
- provide counterparty and settlement evidence if those lifecycle states are to be recorded.

## 7. AERVIND responsibilities

AERVIND will:

- minimize data intake to the agreed cohort;
- preserve source lineage for material inputs;
- make unknown/missing information explicit rather than invent terms;
- reconcile known prior claims and settlements to reduce duplicate-recovery risk;
- challenge positive candidates before claim-ready release;
- require human review before claim-package generation;
- keep generated claim packages `not_submitted` until a real external submission has occurred;
- record external lifecycle states only from real supporting references/evidence;
- maintain separation between unsettled pipeline and verified settlement;
- handle customer information according to the agreed confidentiality/data-handling terms.

## 8. Fixed fee and payment

**Fixed launch fee: EUR 9,500.**

Recommended payment schedule:

- **50% on engagement / scope confirmation**;
- **50% on delivery of the Recovery Scan closeout**.

No success fee is included in the canonical first Recovery Scan unless separately negotiated and documented.

## 9. Timing

Target delivery: **10 business days** from receipt of the complete agreed minimum dataset.

The delivery clock pauses for material customer evidence gaps or contract-interpretation questions that prevent completion of the agreed scope.

## 10. Acceptance standard

The Recovery Scan is complete when:

1. every reviewed candidate has an explicit status;
2. every material calculation input is traceable to a supplied source or recorded as a gap;
3. known prior claims/payments in the supplied scope are reconciled;
4. deadlines are explicit where determinable;
5. false positives are rejected with recorded reasons;
6. claim-ready outputs pass economic, evidence-lineage and human-review gates;
7. external lifecycle events, if any, are supported by real references;
8. the executive closeout does not combine unsettled or blocked pipeline with verified recovery.

A result of **no recoverable claim** is a valid completed engagement when the scoped control closes with evidence-based assurance.

## 11. Exclusions unless separately agreed

- legal opinions or representation as external counsel;
- tax advice;
- statutory/external audit opinion;
- maintenance certification or airworthiness determination;
- unrestricted authority to negotiate or submit claims on the customer's behalf;
- direct ERP/M&E/banking integration;
- whole-fleet transformation outside the bounded scan;
- hosted multi-tenant SaaS deployment.

## 12. Confidentiality and case handling

Before real data is transferred, the parties agree the permitted transfer/storage route and retention/deletion expectations.

AERVIND's standard assisted console processes normalized cohort requests locally/in memory and does not act as the source-document repository. Portable AERVIND case exports may contain confidential normalized financial/evidence metadata and must be stored only in the engagement-approved location.

## 13. Supplier identity and invoicing

AERVIND is the commercial brand. Until a separate company is actually incorporated, the legal supplier in the signed engagement and invoice must be the real Portuguese individual-professional supplier, using the real legal name, tax identity and professional address.

Before signature/invoicing, verify:

- the supplier's real activity registration covers the service;
- the contracting customer legal entity and billing identity;
- customer-specific VAT/invoicing treatment;
- any buyer-specific procurement, insurance or compliance requirements.

If the buyer requires a registered company as a mandatory supplier condition, resolve that requirement before signature rather than inventing or implying a company identity.

## 14. Expansion after the first scan

Expansion is a separate decision after the bounded scan produces evidence. Possible next scopes include:

- additional assets or review periods;
- additional entitlement families;
- deeper forensic reconciliation / claim support;
- recurring monitoring;
- buyer-required integrations or enterprise controls.

Those scopes are separately priced and do not change the fixed EUR 9,500 launch offer retrospectively.
