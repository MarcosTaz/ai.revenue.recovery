### Lease / maintenance context

- Business results / Annual Report 2026 entry: https://corporate.wizzair.com/investors/business-results-calendar/
- Historical annual-report evidence has stated that Wizz leases most aircraft and spare engines and treats recurring maintenance-reserve or lease-end compensation as separate lease-related economics.

Wizz explicitly states that its published Investor Relations contacts are for investment-related inquiries only. Do not use that address for cold commercial outreach.

### Qualification implication

Potential recovery value is high, but internal sophistication and scale can make the first sale harder. Keep the proof narrow and independent.

## Evidence hygiene

Before real outreach:

1. re-check the named buyer is still current;
2. re-check the account-specific trigger is still current;
3. distinguish direct company evidence from secondary reporting;
4. never turn a lease/maintenance signal into an allegation of leakage;
5. record the verification date in the outbound CRM / operating log.
