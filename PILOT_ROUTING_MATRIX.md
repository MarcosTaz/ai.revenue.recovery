# Recovery Radar — Historical Pilot Routing Matrix

Date: 2026-08-29

Status: `PUBLIC RESEARCH / ROUTING PREP ONLY / NOT SENT`

Purpose: separate **historical-case validation fit** from **public routing/access fit**. A strong public case does not imply an easy buyer path, and an easy contact route does not prove a usable historical claim file.

No route below authorizes contact. No named person is asserted to have owned the historical claim unless public evidence says so.

## Decision rule

Use two independent questions:

1. **CASE FIT** — if authorized internal records existed, how strongly could the case test Recovery Radar's wedge?
2. **ROUTING FIT** — is there a current public route that can plausibly direct a historical-pilot inquiry to the right commercial/finance/program owner without inventing an email or misusing a channel?

Do not collapse these into one score.

Recommended authorized route:

`GENERAL BUSINESS INQUIRY → request routing to Strategy & Business Development / Commercial Finance / historical program-recovery owner`.

Do not infer that Matthew Girlando, the CFO, Legal or Driveline personally owned the historical cancellation claim.

Public sources:

**Current decision:** first choice for a T2-quality historical benchmark.

Recommended authorized route:

`MAGNA GENERAL/GROUP INQUIRY → Strategy/Commercial/Finance owner of a closed historical recovery matter`.

Use the indirect-supplier onboarding route only if the commercial approach is explicitly being made as a prospective service supplier and the requested vendor information can be provided truthfully. Do not use it merely to bypass normal routing.

Public sources:

- https://www.magna.com/contact
- https://www.magna.com/contact/groups
- https://www.magna.com/company/suppliers/north-america/supplier-contact-na

**Current decision:** excellent second-wave account after Dauch; procurement/routing complexity is higher than the product's technical pilot requirements.

## Authorization boundary

Until explicit authorization is given, do not:

- submit any form;
- send any email/message;
- call any company;
- request confidential files;
- represent a public accounting amount as a supplier claim value;
- assert that any target has a recoverable claim;
- infer private contact details from names/domains.

The next externally useful action after authorization is a **qualification conversation**, not a data dump and not a live claim pitch.
