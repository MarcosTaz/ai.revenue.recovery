Boundaries

Specialized recovery and proof, with a deliberately narrow first step.

Do you need access to our systems?

No for the first Recovery Scan. A bounded set of customer-approved documents and exports is sufficient to begin. Any later integration is buyer-driven and separately designed.

Is AERVIND an MRO?

No. AERVIND uses customer-provided technical and financial evidence for economic reconciliation; it does not perform maintenance or airworthiness certification.

Does AERVIND provide legal opinions?

No. Contractual rules are configured for economic reconciliation. Matters requiring legal interpretation remain subject to customer or external legal review.

Can AERVIND promise recovery?

No. Outcomes depend on contract terms, evidence, counterparty review and settlement. AERVIND does not guarantee recoverable value.
