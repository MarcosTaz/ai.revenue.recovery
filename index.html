Boundaries

Specialized recovery and proof — without pretending to be something else.

Is AERVIND an MRO?

No. AERVIND uses customer-provided technical and financial evidence to support economic reconciliation; it does not perform maintenance or airworthiness certification.

Does AERVIND provide legal opinions?

No. Contractual rules are configured for economic reconciliation. Matters requiring legal interpretation remain subject to customer or external legal review.

Is this a generic lease-management system?

No. AERVIND is a recovery/proof layer focused on reconstructing entitlement truth and producing controlled recovery outputs.

Can AERVIND promise recovery?

No. Outcomes depend on contract terms, evidence, counterparty review and settlement. The service is designed to identify and prove eligible value, not guarantee it.
