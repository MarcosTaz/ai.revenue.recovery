# Recovery Radar — Historical Pilot Outreach Drafts

Status: `DRAFT / NOT SENT / OWNER AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED`

These drafts are preparation only. They must not be sent, submitted through a web form, or used to initiate external communication without explicit authorization.

The first ask is deliberately **not** “buy software.” It is: identify whether one closed historical cancellation/reduction matter is suitable for a retrospective, evidence-first comparison that cannot affect a live claim.

## Positioning rule

Do not pitch Recovery Radar as:

- AI that decides legal entitlement;
- a replacement for commercial/legal review;
- a claim-generation bot;
- a guarantee of higher recovery;
- a system that knows the customer's contractual liability;
- a generic supplier recovery platform.

Use:

**supplier-side cancellation-claim pre-audit**

A controlled historical assessment asks:

> Given the records that existed at the time, what economic exposure was supported, what entitlement/evidence was missing or ambiguous, what would an OEM auditor likely challenge, and how does that compare with the known historical outcome?
