# Recovery Radar — Paid Historical Validation Pilot

Status: `COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS / NOT SOLD / NOT SENT`

Business truth remains `WTP UNKNOWN` until a buyer performs an independently verifiable price/budget action.

## Offer name

**Cancellation Claim Historical Pre-Audit Benchmark**

## Who it is for

Automotive Tier-1 / Tier-2 suppliers that have a **closed historical** customer program cancellation, terminated purchase order or severe documented volume reduction with program-specific stranded cost exposure.

Best initial cases have at least one of:

- submitted cancellation/recovery claim;
- customer audit/challenge trail;
- settlement or rejection outcome;
- paid reimbursement evidence;
- documented write-off/non-recoverable result.

## What the buyer provides

One authorized historical case, preferably sanitized where practical:

- cancellation/termination/reduction event evidence;
- baseline and revised PO/release/forecast information;
- line-level cost/inventory/tooling/engineering/NRE exposure;
- ownership evidence where material;
- contractual/commercial basis documents and relevant amendments;
- mitigation/reuse/resale/scrap evidence;
- prior credits/reimbursements;
- original claim/internal recovery analysis if one existed;
- historical outcome and reviewer ground truth after Recovery Radar's first-pass analysis has been frozen.

The first pilot does **not** require ERP write access or live system integration.

## What Recovery Radar delivers

1. normalized event/exposure/entitlement evidence graph;
2. duplicate/double-count / mixed-currency / unsupported-value checks;
3. mitigation and prior-compensation reconciliation;
4. independent evidence + entitlement review workflow;
5. line-by-line claimability/review classification with calculation trace;
6. explicit missing-evidence / ambiguity / overclaim map;
7. source-linked draft pre-audit package;
8. deterministic exported package + SHA-256 manifest;
9. retrospective scorecard against independent historical ground truth;
10. FP/FN, state/currency/value error, reviewer corrections and time-to-package measurement;
11. short executive readout: what the system matched, missed, overcalled, undercalled and what would need to change before live use.

## Explicit exclusions

The pilot does not include:

- legal opinion or legal-liability determination;
- external submission to an OEM/customer;
- negotiation or settlement representation;
- physical/on-site asset verification;
- forensic accounting opinion;
- ERP/EDI/PLM production integration;
- promise of higher recovery;
- success-fee claim filing;
- production SaaS access.

If physical inspection was material to the historical claim, that fact is preserved as an external evidence dependency rather than simulated.

## Initial paid-pilot price hypothesis

**€4,500 ex VAT fixed scope for one historical program/cancellation case.**

This is an unvalidated commercial hypothesis, not a market fact.

Rationale:

- public 2026 supplier-audit benchmarks commonly place experienced audit work around roughly €1,000–€2,000 per auditor-day, with a clear remote audit often around €1,500–€4,000 depending on depth;
- Recovery Radar's first pilot includes normalization, independent review structure, deterministic claim package and retrospective measurement rather than a single checklist audit;
- the price is intentionally far below the financial magnitude of the strongest public target cases, while remaining high enough that acceptance would be meaningful WTP evidence;
- a free pilot can still be chosen deliberately for learning, but **must remain classified as learning-only and cannot validate WTP**.

## Fixed-scope boundary

Initial €4,500 hypothesis assumes:

- one customer program/event;
- one primary currency, or currencies separable into independent claim groups;
- up to 1,000 normalized economic exposure lines;
- up to 100 registered evidence records;
- one analyst/source-owner path + one independent reviewer path;
- remote/local evidence review only;
- no custom connector development;
- no live customer/OEM contact;
- no physical inspection;
- one final package + one historical scorecard + one executive readout.

If the records cannot be normalized within this scope, stop and re-scope rather than silently expanding unpaid work.

## Timing hypothesis

Target assisted delivery after a complete usable dataset is available:

- data triage / normalization: 0.5–1.5 operator days;
- evidence + entitlement review: buyer-dependent;
- model/package review and scorecard: 0.5–1 operator day;
- executive readout: <= 1 hour.

This is planning guidance, not a customer SLA until tested on real data.

## Commercial falsification rules

### Strong positive WTP signal

Any of:

- buyer accepts €4,500 fixed price;
- buyer issues PO / approved vendor request for that amount;
- buyer explicitly allocates budget at or above that amount with a named procurement next step;
- buyer pays deposit/invoice.

### Qualified but price-negative

If a buyer confirms pain + data access + historical case but rejects €4,500:

Do **not** immediately discount.

First classify the reason:

- price too high relative to perceived value;
- scope/trust too weak;
- internal process already solves it;
- data preparation burden too high;
- legal/procurement constraint;
- budget timing;
- only interested in live recovery / success-fee economics.

Price should move only after the rejection reason is evidenced.

### Learning-only signal

- free pilot accepted;
- data shared but no budget discussion;
- positive technical feedback;
- repeat-use statement without commercial action.

These do not establish WTP.

### Kill / major pivot signal

After several qualified data-access conversations, materially reconsider the offer if:

- no supplier will provide a closed historical case even under local/sanitized processing;
- buyers report that retrospective pre-audit has no decision value beyond their current spreadsheets/process;
- evidence preparation cost dominates audit value;
- physical/legal negotiation determines nearly all value and the software layer is marginal;
- qualified buyers consistently reject even a narrowly scoped paid assessment for value reasons.
