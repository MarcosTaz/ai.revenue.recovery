# GovMargin pilot offer

## Offer

**Historical Final-Rate & Closeout Reconciliation**

For a federal contractor with flexibly priced contracts, GovMargin independently reconstructs supported contract economics and compares them with cumulative billings and payments.

The pilot is deliberately narrower than “full GovCon consulting.”

## Included pilot scope

- one contractor legal entity;
- up to five federal contracts / task orders;
- up to two completed fiscal years with final-rate or closeout exposure;
- contract + modification register;
- final-rate / provisional-rate reconciliation;
- supported direct-cost / ODC review for supplied categories;
- fee, funding and ceiling reconciliation where terms are evidenced;
- cumulative invoice and payment comparison;
- final-voucher / closeout variance review;
- symmetric underbilling and overbilling findings;
- evidence / rule lineage;
- qualified-review queue for accounting/legal judgment.

## Excluded without separate qualified engagement

- legal advice;
- Contract Disputes Act claim representation;
- claim certification;
- litigation strategy;
- CPA attestation or audit opinion;
- negotiation with DCAA / contracting officer as professional representative;
- unsupported allowability determinations;
- success-fee compensation until legal/professional-practice review clears it.

## Pilot deliverable

One of four truthful outcomes:

1. **NO MATERIAL VARIANCE IDENTIFIED**
2. **SUPPORTED RECOVERY CANDIDATES IDENTIFIED**
3. **PAYABLE / OVERBILLING EXPOSURE IDENTIFIED**
4. **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE / QUALIFIED REVIEW REQUIRED**

Never promise a recovery before the audit.

## Pricing — test hypothesis

### Design-partner pilot

**$7,500 fixed** for the scope above.

Reason for starting fixed-fee:

- separates audit truth from incentive bias;
- avoids assuming contingency compensation is permissible;
- generates real cost-to-deliver data;
- is large enough to test willingness to pay while still below a full bespoke GovCon consulting engagement.

### Expansion hypotheses after pilot data

- $15,000–$35,000 fixed forensic audit for larger contract sets;
- annual continuous-assurance subscription after initial reconciliation;
- PE / M&A portfolio pricing;
- partner wholesale / white-label pricing;
- fixed + success component **only after qualified legal/accounting review confirms permissibility and conflict safeguards**.

These are hypotheses, not validated price points.

## Ideal design partner

Prioritize a contractor with:

- roughly $5M–$50M federal-contract revenue;
- cost-reimbursement / CPFF / T&M / labor-hour exposure;
- settled final rates or aged closeout activity;
- ERP/accounting migration, finance turnover, acquisition or persistent unbilled balance;
- finance leadership willing to provide source records;
- no expectation that GovMargin will manufacture a claim.

## Pilot success criteria

The pilot is successful if GovMargin can:

- ingest and lineage the required records;
- reconstruct at least one complete contract-year deterministically;
- produce zero known false receivables;
- identify both positive and negative variances accurately;
- get client finance / qualified reviewer agreement on calculation lineage;
- measure delivery hours and data friction;
- establish whether the customer would pay for ongoing monitoring.

A pilot with no recovery can still validate the product if it proves accurate economics and avoids a false claim. It does not validate the recovery-frequency thesis.
