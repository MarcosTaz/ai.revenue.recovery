# Recovery Radar thesis kill test — 2026-08-28

## Current result

`SIGNAL / HIGH COMPETITIVE COLLISION / WTP UNKNOWN`

The broad concept **supplier recovery / claim evidence software** is not differentiated enough.

## Material collision evidence

### SupplyWhy

Public product pages position automotive supplier claim recovery around demand changes, inventory exposure, cost impact, claim-evidence assembly and reviewable decision traces. It explicitly leaves contractual liability to finance/commercial/procurement/legal.

This materially collides with `event → evidence → financial exposure → recovery review`, but its public framing is broader operational/planning causality rather than a dedicated cancellation-claim pre-audit for stranded E&O/WIP/FG/tooling/NRE.

Source: https://supplywhy.ai/solutions/automotive-supplier-claim-recovery
Source: https://supplywhy.ai/solutions/oem-forecast-reconciliation

### TRIGO

TRIGO publicly offers evidence-based audits for major program cancellations and describes validating supplier claims for tooling, equipment, inventory and development work, with recoverability/reuse analysis and negotiation-ready evidence.

This is an **exact workflow collision on the buyer/OEM audit side**. It proves the cancellation-claim workflow is real and economically material, but prevents Recovery Radar from claiming novelty around evidence-based cancellation claim auditing itself.

Source: https://www.trigo-group.com/turning-supply-chain-cancellations-into-recoverable-value/

### Stellantis internal claim-audit operation

Stellantis publicly advertises Supplier Claims Auditor / Supplier Claims Audit Manager roles responsible for end-to-end supplier cancellation claim audits, substantive testing, contractual eligibility, inventory/tooling on-site verification, commercial pricing mechanisms and executive reporting.

This proves that OEMs operate a formal adversarial audit function. A supplier-side product must therefore construct a claim that survives this style of audit, not merely total stranded inventory.

Source: https://careers.stellantis.com/job/23243716/supplier-claims-audit-manager/

### Public contractual process evidence

Public automotive terms show that cancellation claims may require prompt submission and supporting data sufficient for buyer auditors to verify and substantiate claimed costs, including records for WIP, raw material and inventory. Public terms also show category-specific limits and exclusions; exposure is therefore not entitlement.

Sources:
- https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1054721/000119312510067132/dex1018.htm
- https://contracts.justia.com/companies/telenav-inc-3726/contract/605064/
- https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/company/supplier-excellence-documents/automotive-special-tooling-procedure.pdf

## Killed positioning

Do not sell Recovery Radar as:

- generic supplier recovery;
- generic claim evidence assembly;
- generic E&O inventory analytics;
- warranty supplier recovery;
- an autonomous legal-liability engine;
- a replacement for physical/on-site verification where the claim requires it.

## Surviving wedge

**SUPPLIER-SIDE CANCELLATION CLAIM PRE-AUDIT**

`CUSTOMER PROGRAM CANCELLATION / SEVERE VOLUME REDUCTION`
→ `STRANDED ECONOMIC EXPOSURE`
→ `CATEGORY / OWNERSHIP / MITIGATION / PRIOR REIMBURSEMENT`
→ `CONTRACTUAL / COMMERCIAL BASIS`
→ `AUDITOR-READY EVIDENCE LINEAGE`
→ `MISSING-EVIDENCE / OVERCLAIM / DOUBLE-COUNT CHECK`
→ `HUMAN COMMERCIAL / LEGAL REVIEW`
→ `CLAIM PACKAGE`
→ `SETTLEMENT`
→ `VERIFIED PAYMENT`.

The product must help the supplier answer **before submission**: “what can we substantiate, what will an OEM auditor attack, what evidence is missing, and what should we not claim?”

## Kill conditions still open

Kill or materially pivot this wedge if evidence shows any of the following:

1. Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers already have a standard, widely adopted product that performs cancellation-claim pre-audit across inventory/tooling/NRE with evidence lineage and mitigation controls.
2. Real suppliers cannot provide exportable inventory/cost/event/contract evidence without prohibitively deep integration.
3. The product adds no material completeness, defensibility or reviewer-time advantage over spreadsheets/current internal process.
4. The same claim outcome depends predominantly on non-automatable physical verification/legal negotiation, leaving insufficient software leverage.
5. Buyers will not pay fixed assessment/success-fee/monitoring economics that support the business.

No commercial proof exists yet.
