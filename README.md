# GovMargin

Government Contract Earned-Revenue & Margin Recovery.

GovMargin is a contractor-side, evidence-first reconciliation system for U.S. federal contracts. It reconstructs what a contractor was legitimately entitled to bill under the contract and applicable rules, compares that amount with cumulative billings and payments, and surfaces both underbilling and overbilling without bias.

## Initial wedge

Historical reconciliation for flexibly priced federal contracts, beginning with settled final indirect-rate true-ups, final voucher / closeout reconciliation, and cumulative billed-versus-allowed differences.

## Non-negotiable safety

- No invented allowability, allocability, entitlement, rates, funding, ceilings, or receivables.
- Positive and negative variances are treated symmetrically.
- Contract applicability and source version must be evidenced.
- Legal/accounting judgment is routed to qualified review.
- Financial arithmetic is deterministic and reproducible.
- A proposed or provisional rate is not treated as a settled final-rate entitlement.

## Current state

`VALIDATED_WITH_MATERIALITY_OPEN`: FAR 52.216-7 and FAR 42.7 prove that final indirect-rate adjustments and cumulative billing updates are real contractual processes capable of producing underpayments or overpayments. Public evidence does not yet establish a representative statistical frequency or average recovery size across the target ICP.
