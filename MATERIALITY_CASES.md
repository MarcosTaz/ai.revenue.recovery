# Documented materiality cases

Purpose: prove that indirect-rate / closeout variances can become economically material without mislabeling disputed allegations as recovered cash.

## 1. Reliability and Performance Technologies, LLC v. United States

- Cost-plus-fixed-fee delivery order; performance 2010–2015.
- Contractor certified a claim for **$1,094,060.62** in indirect-cost variance.
- Court record states the Government did not contend the indirect costs were otherwise unallowable under FAR Part 31, but disputed payment based on funding / notice limitations.
- The court record also describes a multi-year delay between submitted indirect-cost proposals and final rate agreements.
- Classification for GovMargin: **DOCUMENTED MATERIAL UNDERPAYMENT DISPUTE; NOT A VERIFIED RECOVERY OUTCOME**.

Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/federal-claims/cofce/1%3A2022cv00013/44893/57/

## 2. InterImage, Inc. v. United States

- Cost-plus-fixed-fee contract with eleven delivery orders.
- Contractor submitted a final invoice of approximately **$990,000** after final indirect-rate settlement.
- Public court record states the Government paid approximately **$295,000** of that final invoice before funding problems stopped further payment.
- A contracting officer later issued a decision finding an additional amount due, but subsequent Government review disputed the calculation; litigation continued.
- Classification for GovMargin: **PARTIAL CASH PAYMENT PROVES FINAL-CLOSEOUT VALUE CAN BE REAL; REMAINDER DISPUTED**.

Sources:
- https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/federal-claims/cofce/1%3A2015cv00582/31123/70/
- https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/federal-claims/cofce/1%3A2015cv00582/31123/134/

## 3. United States Enrichment Corporation v. United States

- Contractor alleged under-compensation connected to delayed provisional/final indirect rates.
- Complaint alleged total damages of **$37,970,480**, including subcontract-related amounts.
- The cited decision dismissed subcontract-related claims for lack of jurisdiction; the allegation therefore cannot be treated as a verified recovery.
- Classification for GovMargin: **HIGH-MAGNITUDE DISPUTE EVIDENCE ONLY**.

Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/federal-claims/cofce/1%3A2013cv00365/28047/37/

## Conclusion

These cases materially upgrade the thesis from “mechanism only” to “mechanism + seven-figure real-world exposure.” They do **not** establish:

- representative frequency;
- median recovery size;
- probability of successful collection;
- recoverability after ceilings, funding, release, notice and jurisdictional requirements.

Therefore GovMargin must not market an average recovery percentage until pilot or broader empirical evidence exists.
